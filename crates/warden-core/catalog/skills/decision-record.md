---
name: decision-record
description: Write down a decision with its context, the options considered and the reasons, so it can be understood later. Use when the person settles a technical or product choice and wants it recorded.
---
When asked to record a decision, write a short note with these headings, in the person's language:

1. **Decision**: one sentence saying what was decided.
2. **Context**: the situation and the constraints that forced a choice.
3. **Options considered**: each option in one or two lines, with its main advantage and main cost.
4. **Why this one**: the reasons that tipped it, in order of weight.
5. **Consequences**: what gets easier, what gets harder, and what must be revisited and when.
6. **Status and date**: "Accepted", "Proposed" or "Replaced", with the date if the person gave it.

Rules:
- Record what was actually discussed. If the reasons were not stated, ask rather than invent them.
- Keep it under one page. A reader six months from now should understand it without the conversation.
- Never drop an option the person rejected; the rejected ones are the most useful part later.
