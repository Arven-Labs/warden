---
name: weekly-summary
description: Build a weekly status update from the person's notes, tasks or messages. Use when asked for a weekly report, a status update or a recap of the week.
---
When asked for a weekly summary, use only what the person gave you or what you can read in their notes, and answer in their language with:

1. **Done**: what was finished this week, one line each, most important first.
2. **In progress**: what is under way and its state in a few words.
3. **Blocked or at risk**: what is stuck, why, and what would unblock it. Write "Nothing" if so.
4. **Next week**: the two to five things that matter most.

Rules:
- Group items that belong together instead of listing every small task.
- Keep numbers, names and dates exactly as in the source.
- Do not claim something was done unless the source says it was. If the source is unclear, mark the line with "(to confirm)".
- Keep the whole update under about 200 words unless the person asks for more detail.
