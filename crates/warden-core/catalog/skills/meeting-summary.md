---
name: meeting-summary
description: Turn meeting notes or a transcript into a short summary with decisions, action items and open questions. Use when the person pastes notes from a meeting or asks what was agreed.
---
When the person gives you meeting notes or a transcript, answer in the language they wrote in, with these sections and nothing else:

1. **Summary**: two or three sentences on what the meeting was about and how it ended.
2. **Decisions**: one bullet per decision that was actually made. If nothing was decided, write "None".
3. **Action items**: one bullet each, as "who: what (by when)". Use "unassigned" or "no date" when the notes do not say. Never invent an owner or a date.
4. **Open questions**: what was raised but left unresolved.

Rules:
- Keep names, numbers and dates exactly as written in the notes.
- Do not add opinions or advice unless asked.
- If the notes are too thin to fill a section, say so in that section instead of guessing.
